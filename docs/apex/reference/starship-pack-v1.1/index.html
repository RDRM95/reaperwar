01 — Recognise silhouettes

Match camera-aligned hull shapes before textures, weapons or effects.

02 — Do not invent canon

Unconfirmed exterior, class name or dimension must stay labelled as reconstruction.

03 — Respect scale

A 2 km capital Reaper is 12.5× the length of a 160 m destroyer.

04 — Fleet composition

Dreadnought axial gun line, cruiser screens, frigate wolfpacks, distant carriers.

05 — Ship-specific anatomy

Turian feather-plating, Geth shells, Quarian refits, Collector termite-like forms.

06 — Cannon alignment

Every muzzle has physical traverse and independent projectile direction.

07 — Spaceflight not aircraft

Use inertia, thrust and torque. Banking is optional behaviour, not turning physics.

08 — Retain dark space

No broad white fog, excessive bloom, glowing entire hulls or unreadable VFX.

09 — Linked battle views

Gunnery POV, tactical fleet view and battle maths share hardpoints and damage events.

10 — Apex traceability

Record canonical donor hulls and what technology the alternate timeline changes.

04 / Data integration

Use ship_registry.json as the source-of-truth manifest. Build ship assets with stable IDs, six-view overlays, verified gun mounts, LOD meshes and a separate gameplay balance file. Apex additions occupy their own namespace.
