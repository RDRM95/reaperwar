# MASS EFFECT: APEX — ORIGINAL SHIP DESIGN ADDENDUM

All vessels below are **original alternate-universe designs**. Their names, equipment and capabilities are not part of BioWare's original trilogy.

## Systems Alliance — SSV Omaha
Create a super-dreadnought that reads as a direct, decades-long evolution of the actual ME3 Alliance dreadnought. Retain long axial body, machine-plausible engine banks and layered grey-white weaponized armour. The HADES spinal accelerator should be physically integrated through the main structural spine with visible recoil/thermal management. Omega batteries, point-defence mounts and missile cells sit where they can traverse and have uncluttered firing arcs. No Gothic turrets, Star Wars command towers or naval broadside cannon decks.

## Cerberus — Black Fleet
Apex Cerberus has a Destroy-aligned military procurement programme. Ships build on known Cerberus cruiser and Normandy SR-2 design DNA, and on Alliance donor hulls. Subfamilies: stealth command dreadnought, autonomous strike carrier, guided-munition cruiser, special-operations frigate, expendable breaching craft. Keep narrow sensor profile, dark pearl armour, subtle orange trim, hidden hardpoints; don't overdecorate.

## Axiom — 4.2 km Reaper
An ancient operational capital-class Reaper with pale grey weathered plates. The 4.2 km size is Apex canon, not ME3 codex size. His positive influence/anti-indoctrination is conveyed via sound and restrained field behaviour—not bright mystical armour. Retain Reaper joint motion and intimidating weight.

## Krogan and Terminus ship families
Krogan: heavy assault transports, hull reinforcements, robust breach modules; salvage ships from Alliance/turian/other donor shapes first.
Terminus: Aria's coalition combines vessels of mixed ethnic origin, disparate weapon upgrades, industrial scars and fleet identification patches. There is no homogeneous 'Terminus race' geometry.

## Design reviews
Check every concept against a specific donor reference and list 3 retained structural elements. For wildly experimental ships, require plausible Citadel-era technology lineage, unless the story explicitly introduces a new technology.
