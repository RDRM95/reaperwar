# Mass Effect Apex montage production guide

Version 1.0 | 29 October 2026

Purpose: a music-led fan montage that feels like captured gameplay and cinematics from a different Mass Effect trilogy. Recognisable shots remain recognisable. A few altered people, surviving ships and changed military outcomes reveal the Apex history.

Music reference: **Спокойная ночь (Calm Night) — Кино, sped up with looped outro**. Your exact audio file has not been supplied or analysed. The cue map below uses musical landmarks and proportions, not invented timestamps or a claimed beat analysis.

Use the fleet reference for numbers. This guide supplies independent shot cards, edit prompts, motion prompts, captions and a practical Resolve process. It does not require you to create a connected film or recite a plot summary.

## 1 Creative direction

The emotional idea is that the player recognises the trilogy and gradually notices that its losses did not have to happen. The great achievement is a galaxy that learned to prepare, followed by the familiar people finally being allowed to live.

The military triumph must remain expensive. Empty stations on a bridge, rescue shuttles around a wreck, battered surviving ships and interrupted casualty reports earn the final quiet. Preserve the Reapers' scale and lethality. Their defeat becomes greater when they remain formidable.

Humanity provides the initiative, persistence and willingness to commit. The galaxy supplies the combined force. HFY comes from Shepard making other people's best efforts align, and from human ships continuing to fight after their original plan fails.

Avoid filling every frame with captions, huge explosions or interchangeable heroic poses. The music supplies much of the emotion. The most consequential alteration may be Javik standing silently beside a recognisable ME1 terminal.

### Shepard Prime performance

The 2004 Achilles influence belongs in **physical confidence and economy**. Shepard assesses danger without fuss, commits completely and wastes little movement. He can give a brief cold smile when the enemy commits to the trap. He does not become Brad Pitt, wear ancient armour, speak in classical monologues or treat his crew as spectators.

- Keep the recognisable default male Commander Shepard face from your actual reference frames. Preserve the game's head proportions, hairline, eyes, jaw, scars where appropriate and facial animation style.
- His confidence is operational: he already knows his crew, the plan and the fallback. He still asks for reports and listens when somebody knows more.
- In combat he is quick, direct and violent. He uses cover, his squad and actual Mass Effect weapons.
- Renegade decisiveness appears in short commands and refusal to waste lives on a bad position. Paragon leadership appears in who he brings home and whom he trusts with responsibility.
- He does not smirk at friendly deaths, bully allies or give victory speeches over an active tactical channel.
- His strongest final reaction is a held breath released, followed by attention turning back to the people beside him.

**Reusable acting prompt**

> Commander Shepard from the supplied Mass Effect reference, exact in-game likeness. He has the contained physical confidence of a soldier who has prepared thoroughly and intends to survive. Still head, direct eye contact, brief assessment, one economical decision. The expression carries fatigue and certainty of purpose, not certainty of the outcome. His concern for the crew remains visible. Use the original game's facial rig and animation vocabulary. Preserve the reference face throughout.

### The Illusive Man

Keep Martin Sheen's established game-character likeness through the supplied frames. Keep the office, chair, cigarette and controlled manner if they are in the source. Redemption changes what he authorises and whom his fleet protects. It need not change him into an affable Alliance officer.

The Apex divergence removes the earlier compromising artefact exposure. Do not use blackened Reaper veins, control implants or a terminally indoctrinated face as shorthand for the redeemed version. If retaining the familiar blue eye treatment for recognisability, identify it in the production bible as benign AU prosthetics; it is not evidence of immunity.

### Reaper performance

Use the delivery of the original games: measured, declarative, contemptuous and patient. Damage does not turn a Reaper into a shouting human villain. Harbinger can intensify his delivery while preserving that identity.

Show their response through tactics: rotate protection, kill the active gun platform, move destroyers to cover a capital, abandon a damaged harvest ship, withdraw a threatened group. A wounded Reaper still destroys an attacking vessel. Other Reapers continue after Harbinger falls.

A Reaper perspective is an exterior view tied to its line of attack, or a restrained abstract sensor view. Do not invent a humanoid bridge crew, a cockpit full of seats or a human face inside the shell.

## 2 Source and reference preparation

Prefer your own high-quality game recordings and officially released trailers or screenshots. The [official Legendary Edition fan kit](https://www.ea.com/games/mass-effect/community) provides reference imagery and other material. Another creator's cinematic compilation is a separate source; secure permission for substantial reuse.

Create these reference folders:

| Folder | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| 01_Source | Original unmodified clips; game, mission and source recorded in filenames |
| 02_Faces | Shepard front, three-quarter and side frames; neutral and intense expressions; matching costume |
| 03_Characters | Javik, Saren, Nihlus, Jenkins, Miranda, EDI, Legion, Mordin and relevant crew references |
| 04_Ships | Actual Alliance, turian, asari, geth, Normandy, Cerberus, destroyer and capital-Reaper silhouettes |
| 05_Keyframes | Approved still edits; separate start and end frames where needed |
| 06_Generated | Short motion variants; retain model and settings with each render |
| 07_Composites | Masks, clean plates, foreground elements and final shot renders |
| 08_Edit | Resolve project backups, music, subtitle text and export masters |

For each shot, provide only the references it needs. One Shepard face reference, one source composition and one ship reference are usually more useful than a collage of conflicting angles.

Do not rely on a text prompt alone to reproduce a default Shepard face or a capital-Reaper silhouette. An approved still is the identity anchor. If a reference is wrong, fix the still before animation.

## 3 Master prompts

Replace bracketed fields. The shot cards below are addenda to these master prompts. Type captions in the editor after generation.

### A Still editing master

> Edit the supplied frame from [GAME AND MISSION]. Preserve its camera position, lens impression, crop, perspective, character scale, posing, original faces, animation-era geometry, textures, lighting direction, colour response, depth of field and image softness. Apply only this Apex alteration: [SHOT CHANGE]. Match inserted material to the source game's rendering limitations. Maintain the exact supplied Commander Shepard likeness wherever he appears. Preserve all protected characters and established ship silhouettes. Integrate contact shadows, occlusion, reflections and light spill. The result should resemble a frame captured from a modified version of this same game. Leave the image free of newly generated captions, logos and interface text.

### B Short motion master

> Animate the approved edited frame as one continuous [3–5] second shot in the rendering style of [MASS EFFECT 1 / 2 / 3 OR SPECIFIC SOURCE]. Preserve every character's identity, costume, proportions, position and established ship silhouette. Keep the reference camera path, or a nearly stationary camera if no path is supplied. The only new action is [ONE ACTION]. Maintain coherent depth, speed, screen direction and occlusion. Facial movement is restrained. Characters remain silent with no speaking mouth motion. Avoid morphing, new faces, changing uniforms, new spacecraft, repeated explosions, extra limbs, camera orbits and unrequested cuts. Finish on [APPROVED END STATE].

### C Space battle master

> Use the supplied Mass Effect fleet frame and ship references. Preserve the game's actual spacecraft designs and capital-Reaper silhouette. Establish three spatial layers: readable nearby ships, a small number of mid-distance engagements, and a distant fleet suggested by separate drive lights. Show [ONE TACTICAL EVENT] with clear cause and effect. Maintain consistent ship scale and trajectories. Reaper fire remains dangerous and accurately aimed; allied success comes from coordinated attacks. Weapon light, debris and brief impact illumination match the source cinematic. No decorative formations, random beam lattice, planet-sized cruisers, solid wall of ships or unrelated franchise designs. No new on-screen text. Silent shot.

### D Era matching

**ME1 footage:** match the specific original or Legendary Edition capture you supplied. Preserve its simple facial motion, environmental materials, comparatively restrained geometry and characteristic post-processing. A Javik insert needs the same lighting and material treatment as the people beside him. A modern photoreal face in a 2007 cutscene will expose the edit.

**ME2 footage:** preserve the source's lighting range, camera blocking, skin treatment and familiar Normandy/Illusive Man production design. Keep insertions at the same apparent resolution and sharpness.

**ME3 footage:** preserve the selected source's face mesh, armour detailing, motion blur and combat photography. Do not replace it with live-action cinematography just because the instruction says cinematic.

Do not force all three games through one heavy teal-and-orange grade. Consistency should come from restrained final matching, while each game's identity remains readable.

## 4 Independent shot cards

Use a selection. They need not appear chronologically. Captions marked optional can be omitted completely. Durations are usable edit lengths, not promises about a generation model.

### 01 Javik in the original Normandy briefing

**Source:** ME1 Normandy SR-1 briefing room, after the commander has the ship. Choose a wide or a stable reverse angle. **Method:** character insert; 3–4 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Insert Javik at the back of the briefing room, slightly behind Garrus and out of the centre of attention. Use his actual Prothean anatomy and armour. Match ME1 materials, lighting, shadow softness and figure scale. Shepard and every original crew member retain their exact faces. The SR-1's room layout remains unchanged.

**Motion addendum:** Javik watches the display, then makes one small eye movement towards Shepard as Shepard finishes assessing it. The rest of the crew breathes and shifts naturally. No one reacts as though an unexpected guest has appeared.

**Optional caption:** `2183 — PROTHEAN CONTACT CONFIRMED`

**What it communicates:** the galaxy recovered a living witness early enough for him to matter.

### 02 Eden Prime and the patrol that came back

**Source:** ME1 Eden Prime approach, Jenkins running with the team or the stable opening squad frames. **Method:** retain most of the clip; 2–4 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Preserve the familiar terrain and armour. Show Jenkins alive behind cover, receiving a clear hand signal from Shepard. Add Nihlus at the edge of a second angle, covering the team with his actual weapon. The image remains a field operation with existing ME1 assets.

**Motion addendum:** Shepard signals halt; Jenkins stops short of exposure. Cut to Nihlus checking the approach. Keep the action small and immediately legible.

**Optional caption:** none. Recognition is the reward.

**Continuity:** use source portions before the canonical deaths. Do not leave Jenkins' body visible behind his living insert.

### 03 Sovereign at Virmire

**Source:** the actual Virmire Sovereign hologram confrontation. **Method:** preserve the antagonist; change the listening reaction; 3–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Preserve Sovereign's hologram exactly. Add Javik in the background of Shepard's reverse angle, motionless and listening. Shepard appears angry and focused rather than surprised by the existence of the cycle. Keep the original ME1 framing.

**Motion addendum:** The hologram continues its original restrained animation. In the reaction shot Shepard's gaze moves once to Javik, then returns to Sovereign. No gesture of fear or victory.

**Optional caption:** `THE WARNING WAS TAKEN SERIOUSLY.`

**Sound option:** one short original Sovereign fragment can precede the music. Do not rewrite his voice into a theatrical rant.

### 04 A living Prothean at Vigil

**Source:** ME1 Ilos archive and Vigil conversation. **Method:** Javik insert; 4–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Keep the Ilos archive geometry and Vigil's established appearance. Place Javik beside Shepard, a little closer to the projection, with grief held beneath military discipline. Match the ME1 render. Avoid inventing Prothean subtitles or a new interface.

**Motion addendum:** Javik looks over the old machinery, briefly lowers his gaze, and becomes still. Shepard allows him that moment. The projection flickers as in the game.

**Optional caption:** `THIS TIME, SOMEONE WAS THERE TO LISTEN.`

**What it communicates:** the preparation is emotionally connected to previous cycles, not merely a bigger fleet statistic.

### 05 Saren at the tactical table

**Source:** a stable ME1 Saren reference and a Normandy or Citadel briefing angle. This requires a composite rather than a literal existing scene. **Method:** character insert; 3 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Place an identifiable Saren opposite Shepard in a professional briefing. Use an early, minimally augmented reference. His pose is alert and severe. Nihlus is present at the edge of frame. They are working, not shaking hands for a publicity image. Keep turian anatomy and armour from the game.

**Motion addendum:** Saren indicates one point on the map. Shepard studies it and gives a short acknowledgement. No sentimental smile, confession or dialogue animation.

**Optional caption:** `THEIR FIRST AGENT NEVER OPENED THE DOOR.`

**Continuity:** his survival changes how Sovereign must eventually be defeated. Do not silently retain the canonical Saren-death feedback.

### 06 Farixen suspended

**Source:** ME1 Council chamber or ME3 political conference. **Method:** existing dialogue clip plus screen replacement; 2–4 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Preserve the seated representatives and original composition. On a suitable existing display, leave a clean readable area for an editor-added naval mobilisation order. Add restrained archival fleet silhouettes as a background element if the source supports them.

**Motion addendum:** A representative concludes a short gesture. Shepard remains still. The display changes once, with the timing and visual language of that game's UI.

**Editor-added text:** `EMERGENCY NAVAL ACCORD` / `FARIXEN LIMITS SUSPENDED`

**Optional caption:** `THE LIMIT WAS NO LONGER POLITICAL.`

### 07 The Citadel holds against Sovereign

**Source:** ME1 Battle of the Citadel, Alliance approach and Sovereign's exposed position. **Method:** fleet extensions and replacement impacts; 4–6 s split across two clips.

**Still edit addendum:** Preserve the actual Citadel, Destiny Ascension and Sovereign designs. Add prepared Alliance and turian firing positions with clear lines onto the committed capital Reaper. Existing allied losses remain in frame. Sovereign is not pre-emptively powerless.

**Motion addendum:** Several coordinated heavy impacts strike one section. Sovereign fires back, killing a nearby cruiser, before its damaged section fails. Use the original destruction shot only after the new setup establishes a conventional cause.

**Optional caption:** `THE FIRST KILL BECAME A FIRE-CONTROL MANUAL.`

**Continuity:** no Saren corpse, direct-control feedback or implication that all subsequent Reapers share that failure.

### 08 The SR-1 survives the Collector attack

**Source:** ME2 opening attack, using frames before the SR-1 is destroyed. **Method:** substantial space-shot edit; 4–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Retain the Collector cruiser and Normandy SR-1 designs. Add two Alliance escorts at different distances and incoming support from outside the frame. Keep some visible damage on the Normandy. Its crew's danger is real.

**Motion addendum:** An escort breaks the enemy's targeting solution with concentrated fire. The Normandy rolls away and escapes the next shot. Cut before the source clip's fatal hull break.

**Optional caption:** `SHEPARD NEVER LOST THOSE TWO YEARS.`

**What it communicates:** no Lazarus; the head start appears in one recognisable changed event.

### 09 Shipyard rather than miracle

**Source:** ME2 SR-2 reveal, ME3 dockyards and in-game station imagery. These can be separate short inserts. **Method:** ships and industrial plates; 3–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Preserve one foreground ship and the station's architectural language. Add a small number of hulls at distinct construction stages: framework, installed machinery, armour and a departing refit. Include tugs and supply traffic. These are working yards with constrained space.

**Motion addendum:** A tug moves a hull section while a completed escort clears the berth. The scene operates at industrial speed. No fleets materialise fully assembled.

**Optional caption:** `THREE YEARS. EVERY AVAILABLE YARD.`

### 10 The Illusive Man accepts destruction

**Source:** ME2 Illusive Man office, preferably a seated medium close-up and a reverse angle. **Method:** face-preserving edit; 4–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Keep the actual game-character likeness, chair, cigarette and office. Remove any later-stage Reaper corruption from the chosen reference. A small tactical display reflects active allied fleet tracks rather than a control experiment. His expression is reluctant acceptance followed by calculation.

**Motion addendum:** He studies the display, draws once on the cigarette, and closes the file relating to Reaper control. He looks up with the composure of a man committing immense resources. No smile to camera.

**Optional original AU line:** `Then we make certain they can be killed.`

**Optional caption:** `CERBERUS ABANDONED CONTROL.`

### 11 Miranda shares the solution

**Source:** Miranda's ME2 office/briefing material; a separate EDI computer insert. **Method:** screen replacement and reaction; 2–4 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Preserve Miranda's face and uniform. Put a clean tactical transfer interface on an existing screen: Cerberus research delivered to Alliance and turian recipients. A geth recipient can appear in the editor-added metadata. Avoid a glamorous posed portrait.

**Motion addendum:** Miranda confirms the transfer. Cut to EDI acknowledging receipt with a small change in her established interface or face. One operational task, no exposition scene.

**Editor-added text:** `HADES TELEMETRY — COALITION DISTRIBUTION COMPLETE`

**Optional caption:** `THEY SHARED WHAT THEY FOUND.`

### 12 Rannoch before the invasion

**Source:** ME3 Rannoch peace aftermath and quarian/geth fleet imagery. **Method:** preserve actors and alter context; 4–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Retain Tali's actual suit, Legion's established platform and the Rannoch setting. Place Legion alive beside the team in an aftermath frame that otherwise lacks him. Add a modest joint patrol overhead. Keep exact scale and source lighting.

**Motion addendum:** A geth vessel and a quarian escort pass on a common course. Legion turns slightly towards Tali; both return attention to the patrol. The scene is practical cooperation.

**Optional caption:** `THEIR WAR ENDED BEFORE OURS BEGAN.`

**Continuity:** no upload-sacrifice imagery. This settlement does not require Legion's canonical death.

### 13 Mordin after the cure

**Source:** Tuchanka exterior and Mordin laboratory material. Use a new composite for the safe ground aftermath. **Method:** character insert; 3–4 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Keep the established Tuchanka environment. Show Mordin alive at a portable medical station with Wrex in the background. The cure has been deployed through a prepared operation. Do not include the burning Shroud interior.

**Motion addendum:** Mordin checks a result, moves immediately to the next patient, and gives Shepard the shortest acknowledgement. Wrex watches the activity without a speech.

**Optional caption:** `THE CURE WAS READY BEFORE THE TOWER FELL.`

**What it communicates:** planning saved a familiar friend; the cure does not instantly create adult krogan reinforcements.

### 14 Bahak evacuation complete

**Source:** Arrival asteroid control room and Alpha Relay exterior; add separate evacuation traffic beforehand. **Method:** screen replacement plus space insert; 3–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Keep the actual Alpha Relay and Arrival setting. Show the last evacuation convoy already clear before the demolition is initiated. Put the final population-status confirmation on an existing terminal in post. A subsequent wide shows a committed Reaper concentration in the system, with believable separation.

**Motion addendum:** The evacuation status changes to complete. Shepard activates the prepared order. Cut to the relay's destruction without placing friendly ships inside the detonation.

**Editor-added text:** `BAHAK EVACUATION COMPLETE` / `CIVILIAN LOSSES 0`

**Optional caption:** `THEY REACHED AN EMPTY SYSTEM.`

**Continuity:** the fleet has to be physically inside the lethal region; this is an AU ambush, not what the original cutscene already proves.

### 15 Palaven is defended

**Source:** ME3 Menae with Palaven overhead, plus a separate fleet shot. **Method:** restrained sky replacement; 3–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Preserve the soldiers, ground fires and recognisable Palaven image. Add distant allied interception flashes and an organised turian defence line. Damage remains extensive. Do not clean the planet into a spotless blue sphere.

**Motion addendum:** A Reaper weapon fires from orbit; a separate Reaper takes repeated defensive fire and loses its attack alignment. The soldiers keep working. The defence is holding under pressure.

**Optional caption:** `PALAVEN HELD ITS FLEET TOGETHER.`

### 16 Hackett sees the coalition

**Source:** ME3 final fleet departure and Hackett command shots. **Method:** extend existing fleet in layers; 3–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Keep Hackett's exact face and uniform in his reaction shot. For the fleet wide, preserve original ship designs and foreground spacing. Add separate groups with readable human, turian, asari and geth silhouettes. Cerberus arrives as one identifiable formation, not as the entire navy.

**Motion addendum:** Foreground ships hold their vectors while distant echelons complete transit. Hackett studies the report and gives one small nod. No cheering bridge.

**Optional caption:** `38,420 WARSHIPS COMMITTED TO SOL`

**Continuity:** this figure describes the Sol commitment. The image can compress arrivals over time; it must not imply a single instantaneous transit of every ship.

### 17 The Black Fleet joins the line

**Source:** ME3 Cerberus ship imagery, final allied fleet approaches, and an approved Black Fleet reference. **Method:** space composite; 4–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Add a disciplined Cerberus formation into a visible gap beside Alliance vessels. Retain charcoal, white and restrained orange identification, with readable human construction. Place one orange-marked vessel near a turian ship to establish cooperation immediately.

**Motion addendum:** The formation brakes into assigned positions and fires onto a target already engaged by allied heavy guns. Other allied ships maintain course. This was an anticipated reinforcement.

**Optional caption:** `CERBERUS BLACK FLEET — ALLIED IFF CONFIRMED`

### 18 Cerberus protects the Ascension

**Source:** Destiny Ascension battle imagery, Cerberus hull references. **Method:** new tactical interaction built around recognisable assets; 3–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Keep the actual asari flagship at established scale. A damaged Cerberus heavy cruiser moves across the foreground between it and an incoming Reaper attack. An Alliance escort is already helping with recovery. Do not alter the Ascension into a human ship.

**Motion addendum:** The Cerberus ship takes a damaging hit, continues laying defensive fire and exposes the Reaper to the Ascension's return salvo. Cut to the escaping asari ship rather than dwelling on a heroic pose.

**Optional caption:** none. The insignia and action explain the redemption.

### 19 Valkyrie earns its shot

**Source:** approved 2,000 m Valkyrie design, actual capital-Reaper reference and ME3 naval cinematic. **Method:** new space insert; 4–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Set the Valkyrie broadside to the camera, with escorts that establish its capital scale. Its shape retains Normandy lineage. The target Reaper already has a damaged armour section under concentrated allied fire. The firing corridor clears before the spinal weapon discharges.

**Motion addendum:** An escort's torpedo attack disrupts the target's protection. Valkyrie's spinal impact and three heavy Thanix channels strike the same damaged region in a tightly coordinated interval. The Reaper fails at that region; it does not evaporate from untouched condition.

**Optional caption:** `TARGET SOLUTION SHARED. FIRE CONCENTRATED.`

**Continuity:** use the core ship specification. The near-c legacy gun is a separate high-power option, not a hidden default.

### 20 A Reaper meets the line

**Source:** ME3 capital-Reaper attack shot or an approved external reference. **Method:** Reaper-oriented exterior; 3–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Use an exterior camera just above and behind the Reaper's armoured shell, looking down its attack vector. Ahead lies a prepared layered defence, with heavy guns far behind mobile screens. Keep the actual Reaper anatomy. There is no organic cockpit or human interface.

**Motion addendum:** The Reaper kills a foreground escort. The allied formation opens around its wreck and maintains a common firing solution. Converging impacts illuminate the Reaper's armour beside the camera. It shifts to engage the most dangerous battery.

**Optional original AU line:** `Your resistance will be corrected.`

**Optional caption:** `THE FORMATION DID NOT BREAK.`

### 21 Drive attack in the battle chaos

**Source:** approved Cerberus heavy cruiser and capital-Reaper references; ME3 battle lighting. **Method:** a dedicated new shot; 3–4 s usable after setup.

**Still edit addendum:** A crippled Cerberus cruiser with one surviving engine approaches an already contested Reaper on an unoccupied vector far from Earth. Show cooling failures and evacuated dark sections. Other allied ships have cleared the corridor. The silhouette remains human and recognisably Cerberus.

**Motion addendum:** The damaged ship's drive flares blue-white. It accelerates through the cleared line into the Reaper. One abrupt flash interrupts the otherwise continuing battle; armour fragments emerge along the collision vector. The camera is a distant combat view and does not orbit the explosion. Neighbouring enemies manoeuvre and keep firing. Silent.

**Optional caption, before impact:** `DAMPING FAILURE. TERMINAL VECTOR CONFIRMED.`

**Optional caption, in a later tactical insert:** `BLACK FLEET DRIVE ATTACK — CAPITAL KILL CONFIRMED`

**Continuity:** an invented specialist capability. Show one event; do not imply that every civilian freighter can perform it. Keep gigaton/teraton accounting in a tactical overlay or the video description, not in a huge explosion label.

### 22 Harbinger continues fighting

**Source:** the actual Harbinger model and ME3 Earth battle references. **Method:** reference-heavy new shot; 4–5 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Preserve Harbinger's distinct reference silhouette and eye arrangement. Its shell is damaged, not replaced by a generic capital-Reaper mesh. Several allied firing axes converge. One destroyed allied heavy ship drifts through the foreground.

**Motion addendum:** Harbinger rotates, destroys a threatening gunship and attempts to reach a screened exit vector. Allied units shift to cover it. A second coordinated volley strikes damaged machinery. Its limbs lose coordinated support and its weapon cuts out. Other Reapers in the background remain active.

**Optional original AU line:** `This cycle is not concluded.`

**Optional caption:** `HARVEST COMMAND VESSEL — MISSION KILL`

**Continuity:** mission kill is not necessarily confirmed destruction. Confirm the kill in a later shot or keep the caption qualified.

### 23 Shepard looks at the next target

**Source:** ME3 Shepard bridge or cockpit reaction, preferably a three-quarter medium close-up. **Method:** face-preserving performance; 3–4 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Preserve the exact default Shepard face, armour and game rendering. Reflected tactical light changes as a major Reaper disappears from the display. He has a small cut and fatigue, without Lazarus implants. His expression acknowledges the kill for half a second, then returns to the work.

**Motion addendum:** One controlled exhale, a brief glance at Garrus or the tactical officer, then a steady look towards the next contact. Shoulders square naturally. No broad grin or triumphant roar.

**Optional original AU line:** `Mark the next one.`

**What it communicates:** the Achilles influence is confidence under pressure, filtered through Shepard's command responsibility.

### 24 After the war

**Source:** Normandy quiet crew moments, ME1 SR-1 dock or a subdued ME3 observation scene. **Method:** small insert or careful selection; 5–8 s.

**Still edit addendum:** Show Shepard in an ordinary shipboard moment with two or three recognisable crew members. EDI and Legion may be visible in separate cuts, alive and at ease. Retain the actual uniforms, faces and familiar corridor. Through a distant window, intact ships move slowly around a functioning Citadel. The light is ordinary, with no magical coloured wave.

**Motion addendum:** A door opens; someone makes room for Shepard. He notices the conversation and allows himself a small tired smile. He joins them. Hold after the gesture. If using an SR-1 exterior, make its surviving registry readable in post.

**Optional final caption:** `2186 — AFTER THE WAR`

**Optional final Shepard line:** `We get to live.`

**Ending principle:** an ordinary future is the reward. Do not place a Reaper teaser after it.

## 5 Music cue plan

Use the actual waveform to place markers. The proportions below are flexible; the looped outro can be longer than a conventional ending. Do not force the first vocal entrance to a timestamp from another upload.

| Musical region | Approximate share | Useful material | Editing behaviour |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Opening instrumental | 0–12% | SR-1, Javik in 2183, familiar faces alive | Hold recognisable frames long enough for the altered detail to register |
| First vocal passage | 12–32% | Vigil, Council agreement, Saren planning, early yards | Restrained cuts; one short caption every few shots at most |
| First expansion | 32–48% | Rannoch cooperation, Mordin, evacuation, Palaven | Match gestures, ships and direction across eras |
| Stronger central passage | 48–70% | Fleet commitment, Black Fleet, coordinated Reaper kills | Alternate wide tactical views and quiet command reactions |
| Main instrumental/outro rise | 70–88% | Valkyrie strike, a single drive attack, Harbinger under fire | Let impacts land on musical accents; retain brief evidence of friendly losses |
| Looped outro and release | 88–100% | Rescue operations, surviving ships, crew, after the war | Lengthen shots. Reduce captions. End with human-scale life |

This is a mood and intensity map, not a compulsory narrative order. A useful recurring cut is a ME1 preparation image followed by its ME3 consequence, then back to the music-led battle.

### Lean first assembly

Start with ten shots: **01, 04, 08, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20 and 24**. These establish early warning, Shepard's uninterrupted life, Cerberus's change, unity, preparation, conventional resistance and the emotional reward. Add Valkyrie and the drive attack only after that cut works.

The longest shot should not automatically be the biggest explosion. Give the concluding crew moment enough time to settle.

## 6 Optional original dialogue and captions

All lines below are newly written AU dialogue, not quotations from the games. They can be subtitles over silent footage. The montage works without voiced dialogue.

### Shepard

**Fleet call**

> All ships, this is Shepard. Stay with your wing. Keep the firing lanes clear. We finish this together.

**Before the decisive engagement**

> They came for a divided galaxy. Let them see who's here.

**After a costly capital kill**

> Confirm the kill. Get our people out.

**To the Illusive Man**

> You want a future for humanity? Help me give everyone one.

**Quiet final beat**

> We get to live.

The restrained, characteristic version is usually stronger than a long challenge to Harbinger. Shepard does not have to sound like a different franchise to appear at his full potential.

### Hackett

> First reports confirm multiple capital kills. Defence platforms remain operational. All commands, maintain concentration. We can hold them here.

### The Illusive Man

> The Alliance has the firing solution. Give it everything we have.

Or, over the first Black Fleet shot:

> I wanted humanity to have a future, Shepard. I'm committing the fleet.

### Javik

> In my cycle, we learned what they were after our fleets were gone. You learned in time.

### Harbinger

> Your resistance will be corrected.

> This cycle is not concluded.

He should sound convinced of his own purpose even as the tactical evidence changes. Let the next shot answer him.

## 7 Practical Resolve workflow

This is an editor-led process. AI is most useful for a controlled insertion or short new action; existing faces and moving source footage provide continuity.

1. **Build the music timeline first.** Import the exact audio edit, choose a constant delivery frame rate and place markers at instrumental changes, vocal entrances and outro loops. Keep the song unchanged while finding the basic cut.
2. **Choose ten source shots.** Pull recognisable, stable angles with enough space for the intended alteration. Record game, mission, source and source in/out points. A 2 s usable insert is sufficient.
3. **Set the output format before compositing.** A practical default is 16:9 at 30 fps. Use 3,840 × 2,160 only when the sources support it; a consistent 1,920 × 1,080 cut is preferable to visibly uneven upscaling. Capture at a stable rate and conform variable-rate media if it causes drift.
4. **Approve stills.** Export a clean reference frame. Make the smallest necessary edit. Compare Shepard's eyes, hairline, jaw, armour and body scale against the original before proceeding.
5. **Separate moving layers.** Keep unmodified source characters or ships on a protected foreground layer when possible. Create a clean background plate only where the inserted element requires it.
6. **Generate a single small action.** Use the approved image and the relevant motion addendum. Ask for a quiet 3–5 s movement. Generate a short variant before spending time on a long take. Leave text and dialogue out.
7. **Composite locally.** In Fusion, use a Merge for the insert, a Polygon/B-Spline mask for occlusion, and a point or planar track appropriate to the surface. Planar tracking suits screen replacements and roughly planar motion; complex head turns require manual correction or a better source shot. Track, inspect and adjust the matte.
8. **Preserve original faces when possible.** For a simple Javik insertion, restore Shepard and the original crew above the generated plate. This prevents an otherwise good background edit from subtly changing every face.
9. **Match the source.** Adjust black level, saturation, edge softness, light direction and motion blur. Add restrained grain or texture only to match the plate. A sharper inserted face is a defect, not an improvement. Treat SDR gameplay as SDR unless the actual capture metadata says otherwise.
10. **Cut the music montage.** Use hard cuts, brief dissolves and directional matches. Let preparation inserts interrupt battle shots in short bursts. Avoid a long sequence of identical wide shots.
11. **Add captions in post.** Use a clean sans-serif matching the reference subtitle treatment as closely as your available fonts allow. Keep to one or two lines, safe margins and enough screen time to read once. Scale the same subtitle design consistently across every shot.
12. **Handle sound deliberately.** The default is music only. If using dialogue, pick a few short lines and lower the music around them. Do not leave muted source lip movement that looks like a conversation the audience should hear. Avoid clipping and check the final loudest impacts on speakers and headphones.
13. **Export a review master.** Use a high-quality constant-frame-rate format and a separate final upload encode. Watch the full result at normal speed, then inspect every altered face and ship interaction frame by frame around cuts.

Blackmagic's official [Fusion overview](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve/fusion) and [visual effects training guide](https://documents.blackmagicdesign.com/uk/UserManuals/DaVinci-Resolve-20-Fusion-Visual-Effects.pdf?_v=1757574010000) document compositing, tracking and masking. Some automated or advanced features vary by Resolve edition; this workflow does not depend on Magic Mask or a specific paid generation service.

### Capture and identity checklist

- The character is Shepard in the selected game, not an actor replacing him.
- N7 insignia, weapons, hands and armour remain consistent across the cut.
- Javik retains Prothean anatomy, and turians retain turian anatomy.
- Ships maintain silhouette, scale, orientation and drive placement.
- Capital Reapers and destroyers remain distinct.
- An intact SR-1 has not inherited an SR-2 registry or hull.
- The sun, planet or major light source stays on the same side of the shot.
- Fire comes from a plausible weapon location and hits the intended target.
- A claimed kill is visually distinct from a hit, a stagger or a mission kill.
- Friendly ships do not reappear intact after their destruction.
- The final victory contains rescue and aftermath, not only kills.
