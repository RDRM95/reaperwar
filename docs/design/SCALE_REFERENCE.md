## Silhouette notes taken from the chart

- **Sovereign-class:** the body is one long tapering spine (roughly 55–60 % of the overall length) rising from a bulky, layered "hood" where the arms root; five to six articulated arms/legs account for the remaining 40 %; the shell is near-black graphite with cold dark-blue sheen, plating in overlapping bands, thin pale-blue seams; no brown tones. Overall proportion is tall and narrow: the body width is well under a tenth of the length.
- **Harbinger:** same family, slightly broader hood, longer and more splayed arms, more pronounced ridges along the spine.
- **Destroyer:** a compact four-legged version with a stubby spine; the legs dominate the silhouette.
- **Kilimanjaro-class:** a long, flat, wide slab; the spinal gun housing runs the full length under a stepped dorsal superstructure; the bow tapers to a wedge; pale hull with blue stripes along the flanks; sponsons visible along the sides.
- **York-class cruiser:** the same slab language at ~70 % of the dreadnought's length, with a raised stern block and a forward wedge; blue-white livery.
- **Berlin-class cruiser:** slimmer, with red accent stripes; angular bow.
- **Relative scale to target in-game:** Sovereign : Kilimanjaro = 2 : 1 in length and far bulkier; Kilimanjaro : York = 1.4 : 1; cruiser : Normandy ≈ 3.3 : 1; destroyer ≈ 0.8 × Normandy.
